Queensland-ready in eight moves

Build the evidence before the warranty.

The Supplier Code applies across Queensland Government procurement regardless of value. Treat Code compliance as an operating system, not a box to tick at submission.

1

Register

Create the free Supplier Portal profile. Set useful categories and real contacts. Use QTenders, eTender and the forward pipeline separately.

2

Self-assess

Check the 2026 Supplier Code across legal compliance, ethics, workplace practices, human rights, environment, conflicts and supply chains.

3

Retain proof

The Procurement Assurance Model can test the evidence. Keep registers, policies, records and corrective actions current.

4

Check the scheme

Building, maintenance, civil, road, bridge, asphalt and specialist work may need PQC, QBuild, TMR or National Prequalification before the bid.

5

Price the contract

Model labour, materials, travel, mobilisation, reporting, insurance, retention, security, delays and payment timing.

6

Offer public value

Answer the outcomes in the tender with specific people, suppliers, dollars, dates and measures. Do not paste generic social-value claims.

7

Flow it down

Prime contractors put Code obligations and measurable purposeful-procurement commitments into subcontracts and monitor them.

8

Control delivery

Track licences, insurance, WHS, quality, environment, records, complaints, payment and subcontractor performance through the contract.
