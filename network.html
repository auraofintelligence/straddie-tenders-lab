Simulate before procurement.

Film club tools, cinematic-universe work and game-world/web3 simulacrum ideas can rehearse possible futures before they become formal grants or tenders. They are theorised and prototype-facing, not implemented operating systems. If one of those futures starts to become fundable or procurable, this lab helps ask: what is real, who has authority, what vehicle can qualify, and what needs to be ready before anyone applies?
