First Nations procurement can open real doors, but identity, Country, cultural knowledge and partnership claims must be handled with care.

Commonwealth eligibility from 1 July 2026

Ownership and control must be real.

For the Indigenous Procurement Policy, the strengthened definition is at least 51% Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander owned and controlled, or an eligible corporation registered with ORIC. NIAA also provides a 2026–27 transition for businesses that meet the previous 50% ownership definition.

Build the proof

  • Current ownership and beneficial-interest records.
  • Board, voting, management and decision-control records.
  • ORIC registration where that is the chosen structure.
  • Accurate Indigenous business directory and supplier profiles.
  • Subcontract workshare, payment and participation records.

No black cladding.

Do not put an Indigenous name, shareholder or partner on the front of a bid while control, profit and delivery sit elsewhere. Build an enterprise with real ownership, governance, work and benefit.
