Aura Scan to Twin

Site capture becomes reviewable tender evidence.

The current Aura Local Scan codebase is the foundation. The tender-facing product name and workflow is Aura Scan to Twin.

1

Capture

Record the bowl, surrounding levels, drainage clues, defects, access and measured controls with iPhone LiDAR and site photography.

2

Preserve

Keep the original .aurascan package immutable with metadata, checksums and a clear public/private evidence boundary.

3

Process

Clean, align and inspect the scan on Windows. Produce point cloud, mesh, section and measurement outputs with control checks.

4

Hand off

Deliver GLB/OBJ/PLY/LAS/PCD/USDZ, DXF sections, reports and source references to VFG and the qualified design reviewers.

Proven in the current pipeline

Working production capability

  • Windows ingest, cleaning, multi-scan processing, reconstruction and scale control.
  • Mesh, point-cloud, USDZ and R2018 DXF output paths.
  • Immutable evidence packages, checksums, safe transfer and reviewed public/private bridge.
  • Synthetic skate-asset control proof and a Windemere evidence-bridge record.

Field proof to close

Before relying on it in the bid

  • Build and sign the iOS app on Apple hardware.
  • Run a real iPhone LiDAR capture at the relevant site or a controlled equivalent.
  • Compare key dimensions with an independent measurement method.
  • Have the right specialist accept any tolerance used for survey, structure, access, fabrication or construction.

The product claim is strong and specific.

Aura Scan to Twin is a capture, evidence and design-handoff system. It reduces site ambiguity and makes specialist review faster. It does not replace the professional authority required for engineering, survey or construction acceptance.
