Commonwealth-ready in eight moves

Make the business legible to a national buyer.

The current Commonwealth Procurement Rules took effect on 17 November 2025. The Supplier Portal became available to all businesses through AusTender in July 2026.

1

Register

Create AusTender alerts and a Supplier Portal profile. Keep ownership, size, location, categories and contacts accurate.

2

Define the business

Know whether the entity meets the CPR definition of an Australian business and whether it is an SME with fewer than 200 full-time-equivalent employees.

3

Read the whole pack

The ATM, draft contract, schedules, forms, security clauses and addenda form one job. Build the matrix before drafting prose.

4

Run the triggers

Test value, category, location, staff numbers, Indigenous participation, tax, skills, environment, payment times and industry participation.

5

Pass the Code

Prepare evidence for ethics, conflicts, governance, tax, records, WHS, workplace rights, human rights, environment, security and subcontractors.

6

Choose the role

Lead only if the entity can carry the contract. Otherwise offer a defined work package to a prime, panel member, consortium or joint venture.

7

Price delivery

Include reporting, security, travel, assurance, subcontract management, payment timing, insurances and contract risk.

8

Approve and lodge

Named people approve technical claims, partner evidence, price, declarations and departures. An authorised person lodges and retains the receipt.

Official rules: procurement thresholds and procurement-connected policies.

Australian business

Keep the definition evidence ready.

A Commonwealth Australian business must meet the ownership or Australian-market-listing test, be an Australian tax resident and have its principal place of business in Australia.

Security

Match the control to the contract.

Government information, systems, people, facilities and Defence work can add PSPF, Essential Eight, clearances, IRAP or DISP requirements. Claim only the controls and memberships the entity can prove.
