Build a company stack, not shell companies

One strong operating vehicle. Several clear delivery lanes.

Start with an operating company that can employ people, hold IP, insure the work and subcontract. Add a project company or joint venture only when ownership, finance and risk make it useful.

The Gumpi preparation run.

  1. Track the TMR project page, QTenders, forward pipeline, industry briefings and relevant awarded contracts.
  2. Break the concept into likely packages: marine structures, civil works, buildings, shelters, access, parking, landscape, systems, environmental and handover.
  3. Map the likely PQC, TMR, National Prequalification, QBCC, RPEQ, marine, environmental, WHS and financial-capacity requirements by package.
  4. Choose the two or three packages where local capability can be excellent. Build proof there first.
  5. Approach qualified primes with a one-page capability statement, evidence pack, delivery price logic and a precise workshare proposal.
  6. Run both Queensland and Commonwealth policy triggers because the project is jointly funded. The final procurement lead and tender pack decide what actually applies.
  7. Use the terminal work to build reusable people, systems, tools and records for the next transport and infrastructure bid.

Likely public-project gates

Prepare now.

  • Supplier Code and Procurement Assurance evidence.
  • Purposeful procurement commitments flowed into subcontracts.
  • Construction and transport prequalification by work package.
  • Financial capacity, insurance and project cashflow.
  • Indigenous participation and authentic ownership/control where claimed.
  • Environmental, cultural heritage and protected-area controls.
  • Data, cyber, asset handover and records requirements.
